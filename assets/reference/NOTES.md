# EUC riding reference notes

Sources (videos are git-ignored, re-download with yt-dlp):
- `video/carving_howto.mp4` — "EUC Carving. How to carve?" (Adrenaline Addicted), youtube.com/watch?v=fM15VBRieZI
- `video/dc_group_ride.mp4` — "DC's EUC Group Ride (Carving at its Best!)" (Vee), youtube.com/watch?v=0wQh0mSIluE
- `hard_stop.webp`, `curb_pop.webp` — stills supplied by the user

## Cruise (straight, any speed) — carving 3:25–3:45
- Upright torso, knees softly bent, shins against the pads.
- Arms relaxed, hanging slightly away from the body, hands near the hips. Not stiff.

## Slow turn, ~10 km/h — carving 1:05–1:16 (red arrows)
- Wheel tilts into the turn (driven by the legs/ankles).
- Shoulders/upper body shift the *opposite* way: counter-lean. Hips stay roughly over the wheel.
- Torso stays fairly upright; arms near the body, slight balancing movement.

## Fast carve, 30+ km/h — carving 2:13–2:25, 2:58–3:03 (red arrows)
- Deep crouch: knees strongly bent, thighs approaching horizontal, hips low.
- Knees, hips AND shoulders all pushed into the turn (arrows all point inward) — body and wheel lean together.
- Torso leans forward and into the turn.
- Inside arm reaches down low toward the inside knee/ground; outside arm bent, held up near the chest.

## Wrong technique — carving 1:38–1:45 (red X)
- Leaning the wheel at speed while standing tall with straight legs and an upright torso. Looks stiff
  (this is roughly what the mod does today).

## Hard stop — hard_stop.webp
- Deep squat, hips dropped and pushed back behind the axle; shins pressed into the pads.
- Torso compact, only slightly forward. Arms thrown up and out, elbows ~90°, hands at head height.
- Wheel slightly nose-up.

## Curb pop / airborne — curb_pop.webp
- Knees pulled up toward the chest, wheel lifted with the legs (clamped between the shins).
- Torso folded far forward, head low looking at the landing.
- Arms forward and slightly down, elbows bent, fists in front around knee/chest height.
